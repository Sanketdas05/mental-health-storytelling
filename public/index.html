We spend so much of our lives polishing the work, managing impressions, and making sure nobody hears the fatigue in our voices.

It feels easier to type “I’m fine :)” than to burden someone with the honest, tangled truth.

Carrying everything alone in the dark isn’t strength. It’s just exhaustion with good posture.

If anything on this desk felt painfully familiar, give yourself permission to stop performing. The people who care about you don’t need you to be smooth and unshakeable. They just need you here.
